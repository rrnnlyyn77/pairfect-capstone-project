IDENTIFY YOUR SKIN TYPE AND ITS NEEDS

What type of skin do you have?

❮

SKIN TYPE: OILY

DOs

  • Use lightweight, oil-free moisturizers
  • Choose salicylic acid for unclogging pores
  • Use non-comedogenic products

DON'Ts

  • Avoid heavy or greasy products
  • No over-exfoliating (causes more oil)
  • Avoid pore-clogging makeup

SKIN TYPE: DRY

DOs

  • Use thick, hydrating moisturizers
  • Include hyaluronic acid and ceramides
  • Use gentle, non-foaming cleansers

DON'Ts

  • Avoid stripping cleansers
  • No harsh exfoliants
  • Avoid alcohol-based toners

SKIN TYPE: NORMAL

DOs

  • Maintain a balanced routine
  • Use SPF daily
  • Hydrate consistently

DON'Ts

  • Don't skip moisturizer
  • Avoid overusing strong actives
  • Don't overwash the skin

SKIN TYPE: SENSITIVE

DOs

  • Use fragrance-free, gentle products
  • Patch-test new skincare items
  • Look for soothing ingredients like aloe or centella

DON'Ts

  • Avoid strong actives (retinol, high % acids)
  • No physical scrubs
  • Avoid alcohol-heavy formulas
